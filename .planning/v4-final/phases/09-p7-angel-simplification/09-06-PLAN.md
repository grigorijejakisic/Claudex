point. Phase 4.1 split curation into specialized sweeps: feedback density
→ critical_rules, multi-project boost, shape vocab promotion, and the
/endsession lesson-proposer flow. Those live in distinct modules
(`feedback-promoter.ts`, `multi-project-marker.ts`, `shape-vocab-promoter.ts`,
`lesson-proposer.ts`, `pointer-recall.ts`) wired into heartbeat Phase 5c
(line ~549). The legacy `runProactiveCuration` became orphan infra at Phase
4.1 close.
