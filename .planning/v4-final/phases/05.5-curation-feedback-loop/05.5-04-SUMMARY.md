# Phase 5.5 Plan 04: Heartbeat Curation-Feedback Sweeps Summary

Two heartbeat-driven sweeps close the curation loop:
- **Archive** (24h cadence): demotes lesson pointers with 0 retrievals in 90d AND no helpful_yn=1 to `tier='background'`.
- **Promote** (7d cadence): bumps `last_fired_at_epoch` on lesson pointers with ≥3 recalls AND ≥1 helpful_yn=1 to today, with a 24h cooldown to prevent thrash.

Re-promotion implicitly un-archives — a previously-archived lesson that re-earns recalls climbs back to foreground.
