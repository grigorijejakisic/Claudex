## Investigation outcome

### Root cause (named, not "TBD")

**Two distinct failure modes** combine to produce the reach gap:

1. **Enqueue gap (~107 sessions)**: Sessions reach `status='completed'` without firing `memory_curation_pending`. Producers are correctly wired (session-end hook line 47 + Angel auto-close heartbeat line 191), but historical sessions pre-date the enqueue mechanism + CC-shutdown-crash edge cases miss the enqueue.
2. **Drain backlog (~7 sessions)**: 23 enqueued events still pending vs 16 done. Phase 5b drain reliability concern.

### Pre-existing bug confirmed

100% of the 24 existing transcript_chunk rows carry 10-digit-second timestamps. Plan 01's `normalizeEpochMs(row.created_at_epoch)` makes them comparable against ms-precision peers at read time. No data migration.
