<objective>
Polish the Vesna probe suite to close the name-vs-implementation gap on two categories. This is standalone work (no dependency on W1 or W2 foundation).

**The two problem categories identified in the 2026-05-10 probe audit:**

1. **`lesson-application` (3 probes):** The discriminator question is: "if you remove the directional language from the artifact's summary, does the probe still pass?" If yes, the probe is entity-recall in disguise. Each probe must be audited and either confirmed, recategorized, or rewritten.

2. **`deliberation-engagement` (5 probes):** These probes test for `## Deliberation Surfaced` markdown in the assembly output — that is, they test whether the *pipeline* injected the deliberation surface, not whether the *agent* engaged with it. Two valid responses: (a) rename to `deliberation-pipeline-fanout` to match what the regex tests, AND (b) author new `deliberation-agent-engagement` probes that test what the original name promised.

**What is NOT changing:** entity-recall, constraint-recall, handoff-pickup, cross-project, self-instrumented — these five categories are correctly binary and are not touched.
</objective>
