## Decisions Made
- Quality gate fail-closed on error (reject malformed input, not pass garbage)
- emitErrorTelemetry is the right abstraction (emit helper, not operation wrapper)
- writeCheckpoint: INSERT pending before reads for traceability
- OpenClaw and CC must stay completely separated — no coupling, no bridges
- Paperclip is the orchestrator — heartbeat-driven, structured tasks, human oversight
- CC drives the conversation with users (via GSD discuss), Echo is formatting layer
- Chell abandoned for this use case (app quality too low, SSH+Tailscale is better)
- Structured task specs at agent boundaries, not natural language delegation
